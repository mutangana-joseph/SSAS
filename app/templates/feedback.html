Sangiza abandi ubunararibonye bwawe mu buhinzi

Ibyo abahinzi bahura na byo mu mirima yabo bishobora gufasha abandi gusobanukirwa neza n'ubwoko bw'ibihingwa. Tanga igitekerezo cyawe hano.

Tanga igitekerezo cyawe

Uzuza amakuru akurikira uko ushoboye. Andika ibyo wiboneye mu murima wawe.

ℹ️

Impamvu igitekerezo cyawe ari ingenzi

Ubunararibonye bw'abahinzi bushobora gufasha kumenya ibibazo n'amahirwe yo guhinga mu turere dutandukanye. Icyakora, igitekerezo cy'umuhinzi kimwe nticyemeza ko ubwoko bw'igihingwa bukwiriye akarere kose. Amakuru agomba kugenzurwa no kugereranywa n'ibindi bimenyetso.
