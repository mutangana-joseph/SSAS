AHO UHEREREYE

Hitamo aho ushakira abatanga imbuto

Tangira uhitamo akarere, hanyuma uhitemo umurenge uri muri ako karere.

ℹ️ Umurenge uzajya ugaragaza gusa imirenge iri mu karere wahisemo.

IBISUBIZO

Abatanga imbuto babonetse

Hano hazagaragara abatanga ubwoko bw'igihingwa wahisemo, hakurikijwe aho uherereye.

🧑‍🌾

Nta bisubizo biragaragara ubu

Banza uhitemo ubwoko bw'igihingwa, akarere n'umurenge. Abatanga imbuto bazajya bagaragara hano amakuru yabo amaze kubikwa muri SSAS.

🌿

Inama ku bahinzi

Banza ugenzure ko ubwoko bw'imbuto ugiye kugura bujyanye n'ibyo ukeneye. Kuba umucuruzi agaragara muri SSAS ntibihita byemeza ubwiza bw'imbuto cyangwa ko zikwiye guhingwa mu karere kawe.
