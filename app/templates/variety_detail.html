Menya byinshi ku bwoko bw'igihingwa

Hano usanga ibisobanuro by'igihingwa, amakuru ajyanye n'uko gishobora guhingwa mu karere kawe, n'uburyo bwo kubona abagurisha imbuto cyangwa ibindi bikoresho.

☀️ 🌿
🌱
IGIHINGWA

Ibishyimbo

Ubwoko bw'ibishyimbo (urugero)

Ubu ni urugero rw'ubwoko bw'ibishyimbo bwashyizwe muri SSAS kugira ngo tugaragaze uko amakuru y'ubwoko bw'igihingwa azajya agaragara. Ibisobanuro nyakuri bizashyirwamo nyuma yo kubona amakuru yizewe.

🌾 Igihingwa: Ibishyimbo 📍 Akarere: Kazatoranywa
AMAKURU Y'ISUZUMA

Ese ubu bwoko bukwiranye n'akarere kawe?

Reba amakuru y'isuzuma mbere yo gufata icyemezo cyo guhinga ubu bwoko.

🔎
Imiterere y'isuzuma

Buracyasuzumwa

Nta mwanzuro wizewe urashyirwa hano werekana ko ubu bwoko bukwiriye cyangwa budakwiriye akarere runaka. Hakenewe amakuru n'ibimenyetso byizewe mbere yo gutanga umwanzuro.

Ibimenyetso: Amakuru y'isuzuma azongerwamo igihe azaba yabonetse kandi yagenzuwe.
UBUMENYI MU BUHINZI

Amabwiriza yo guhinga

Iki gice kizajya kigaragaza intambwe zo guhinga ubu bwoko, uhereye ku gutegura ubutaka kugeza igihe cyo gusarura.

01

Gutegura ubutaka

Amabwiriza yizewe ajyanye no gutegura ubutaka bw'ubu bwoko azashyirwa hano.

🌍
02

Gutera imbuto

Igihe cyo gutera, intera hagati y'imirongo n'iy'ibihingwa bizongerwamo hashingiwe ku mabwiriza yemewe y'ubu bwoko.

🌱
03

Kwita ku bihingwa

Amakuru ajyanye no kuhira, kurwanya indwara n'udukoko, no kwita ku bihingwa azashyirwa hano.

💧
04

Gusarura

Igihe cyo gusarura n'ibimenyetso byerekana ko igihingwa gikuze neza bizongerwamo hashingiwe ku makuru yizewe.

🧺
ℹ️

Icyitonderwa: Aya makuru ni urugero rw'imiterere y'urubuga. Si amabwiriza yemejwe yo guhinga ubu bwoko. Amakuru nyakuri azashyirwa muri SSAS amaze kugenzurwa.
